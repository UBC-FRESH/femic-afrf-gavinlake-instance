# Gavin Lake planted regeneration curves

One FVS-BC bare-ground planted case per productive AU, 79 AUs total.

## Method

- `NOTREES` + `ESTAB` + `PLANT 1 <species> 450 95 2 1 0`
- species = the AU's dominant FVS species
- site index = the AU's FVS site index
- 100 years, ten-year cycles
- summary converted from BC imperial to TPH, m2/ha, m3/ha, and metres

## Result

- 79 successful runs
- 869 curve rows
- mean 100-year planted volume: **480.4 m3/ha**
- mean 100-year basal area: **46.7 m2/ha**
- mean top height: **34.4 m**

This is a planted bare-ground reference, not a regeneration survey. It has one
species, no natural ingress, no brushing, and no competition control.
